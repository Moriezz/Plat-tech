Product Specifications





1.Azul Aglow CC Cream addresses redness, uneven skin tone, and flaws to provide a faultless base for makeup application or a glowing unmade-up appearance.

2.Radiant Glow: Your skin seems naturally bright and revitalized thanks to the exclusive formula's radiant glow.

3.Enjoy the versatility of buildable coverage, which lets you go from a sheer, dewy finish to a more sophisticated, polished look.

Ingredients:

1.Niacinamide (Vitamin B3): Azul Aglow CC Cream, which is enhanced with niacinamide, helps to smooth out fine wrinkles, enlarged pores, and dullness, resulting in a complexion that looks younger and smoother.

2.Licorice Extract: By reducing hyperpigmentation and uneven skin tone, the addition of licorice extract brightens the skin tone.

3.Hyaluronic Acid: Packed with hyaluronic acid, this CC cream leaves your skin feeling hydrated and lush.

How to use:

1.Prepare Your Skin: Wash and moisturize your face first.

2.Apply the CC Cream: Using a makeup sponge or your fingertips, lightly dab some Azul Aglow CC Cream onto your skin. Apply evenly and blend gently, working your way outward from the centre of your face.

3.Build for preferred Coverage: Whether you're going for a more polished finish or a natural, radiant look, layer the CC cream as necessary to reach your preferred degree of coverage.

4.Use Alone or as a Base: Azul Aglow CC Cream can be used as a perfecting base before adding other cosmetic products, or it can be worn alone for a radiant, makeup-free look.

Product Specifications



Features:

1.Rich, Velvety Pigments: Azul Majestic Lipstick has pigments that are rich and velvety, giving you powerful, saturated colour in just one stroke. This means that every time you apply it, your lips will look stunning.

2.Long-lasting Comfort: You can enjoy a magnificent lip look that lasts for ages thanks to the opulent formula's long-lasting wear without sacrificing comfort.

3.Creamy Texture: Your lips will feel luscious and the application will be flawlessly even thanks to the creamy, smooth texture that applies so easily.

Ingredients:

1.Argan Oil: Azul Majestic Lipstick, which is enhanced with nourishing argan oil, keeps your lips nourished and conditioned, reducing flakiness and dryness.

2.Vitamin E: Packed with of antioxidants, this lipstick shields your lips from environmental aggressors and makes them feel supple and soft.

3.Hydrolyzed Silk: Adding hydrolyzed silk results in a posh finish that leaves your lips feeling incredibly soft and richly embellished.

How to use:

1.Prepare Your Lips: For the greatest application, start with lips that are hydrated and clean.

2.Define with Lip Liner: For a more accurate application, you may choose to use a lip liner to define the features of your lips.

3.Apply Lipstick: Using a circular motion, apply Azul Majestic Lipstick straight to your lips, working your way outward from the centre.

4.Blot for Longevity: To prolong the wear of your lipstick, lightly dab your lips with a tissue and reapply it for more colour intensity.

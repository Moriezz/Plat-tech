Product Description

Unleash the power of captivating lashes with Azul Enviable Mascara. Elevate your eye makeup routine with this mascara designed to define, lengthen, and volumize for lashes that command attention. Experience the envy-inducing allure of Azul's latest mascara innovation.

Product Specifications

Features:

1.3-in-1 Performance: With its potent formula that combines volumizing, defining, and lengthening properties, Azul Enviable Mascara gives you complete control over the appearance of your lashes.

2.Intense Colour Payoff: Get a jet-black, rich finish that accentuates your lashes' inherent beauty, bringing your eyes to life and leaving a lasting impression.

3.Curl-lock technology is a special ingredient in the recipe that lifts and holds lashes in place to give the appearance of wide eyes and fanned lashes that lasts all day.

4.Application Without Clumps: The precision wand separates and coats every lash uniformly, eliminating clumps and guaranteeing a perfect finish each and every time.

Ingredients:

1.Argan Oil: Azul Enviable Mascara, which is infused with nutritious argan oil, conditions and fosters a luscious, glossy appearance in addition to improving the health of lashes.

2.Vitamin E: Packed with the antioxidant-rich vitamin E, this mascara protects against environmental stressors and maintains the vitality of the lashes.

How to use:

1.Wiggling the wand back and forth, begin at the base of your lashes and coat each lash from root to tip.

2.While the mascara is still wet, add another coat to get the desired lash thickness for extra volume.

3.Gently wiggle the wand at the base of the lashes and sweep upward for a lifted impression; concentrate on the outer corners for a subtle cat-eye look.

4.If necessary, use an eyelash comb to divide your lashes for a neat, defined appearance.

Packaging:

The sleek, matte-black tube that holds the Azul Enviable Mascara features the Azul emblem for an elegant touch. The sleek form makes handling simple, and the tight seal keeps the mascara fresh. With Azul Enviable Mascara, you can effortlessly combine performance and glamour in one swipe.

Product Specifications



Features:

1.Moisturizing Elegance: The moisturizing nature of Azul Ethereal Lip Balm glides on smoothly and gives your lips an instant boost of hydration, leaving them supple, kissable, and soft.

2.Subtle Tint: Without compromising the comfort of a lip balm, experience the ideal balance of a subtle tint that accentuates your natural lip colour and adds a hint of ethereal beauty.

3.Long-lasting Comfort: This nourishing formula keeps your lips from drying out or chapping for an extended period of time, making it a necessary tool for every season.

Ingredients:

1.Shea Butter: Azul Ethereal Lip Balm, enhanced with shea butter, leaves lips feeling incredibly hydrated and well-conditioned, encouraging smooth, velvety lips.

2.Jojoba Oil: Jojoba oil is a lightweight, non-greasy ingredient that gives your lips the nutrition and health they need to be healthy.

3.Vitamin E: Packed with antioxidants, this lip balm protects your lips from environmental aggressors while preserving their natural beauty.

How to use:

1.Apply liberally: Apply Azul Ethereal Lip Balm on your lips by gliding it there from the middle outward to the corners.

2.Build for Intensity: Apply extra lip balm layers until the colour you want is as intense as you like.

3.Ideal as a basis: For a smoother, more radiant finish, use as a hydrating basis before applying your preferred lipstick.

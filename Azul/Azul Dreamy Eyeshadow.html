Product Description

Savour the ethereal charm of Azul Dreamy Eyeshadow, a palette that goes above and beyond the norm with a captivating array of tones to spark your imagination. Let your creative side out and enhance your eyes with rich, pigmented shades that mix together to create ethereal, alluring looks.

Product Specifications

Features:

1.Versatile Colour Selection: The Azul Dreamy Eyeshadow Palette comes with a carefully chosen range of colours that are perfect for creating a variety of eye-catching looks. These colours range from strong, sparkling tones to gentle neutrals.

2.Extremely Pigmented: Get a rich colour payoff with each brushstroke, guaranteeing vivid, long-lasting eyeshadow that shines from day to night.

3.Buttery Texture: Each eyeshadow's silky, buttery texture makes application easy to work with, enabling blending and layering without effort.

4.Buildable Intensity: With the buildable formula, you may adjust the intensity to fit your mood, whether you're more into a dramatic, seductive gaze or a gentle wash of colour.

Ingredients:

1.Jojoba Oil: Azul Dreamy Eyeshadow's nurturing jojoba oil infusion not only improves the blendability of the eyeshadow but also nurtures the sensitive skin around your eyes, leaving it feeling nourished and smooth.

How to use:

1.Apply the base or primer of your choice to prepare a smooth surface for applying eyeshadow.

2.Apply the colour of your choice to your eyelids using an eyeshadow brush, blending it outwards from the inner corner.

3.Try varying the textures and hues to add volume and depth.

4.Apply many colours or moisten your brush before applying for a more dramatic effect.

Packaging:

Dreamy artwork adorns the elegant and compact palette of Azul Dreamy Eyeshadow, perfectly encapsulating the essence of the product. The palette has a wide mirror for application while travelling and a tight clasp to keep the pigments safe. Accept the enchantment of Azul Dreamy Eyeshadow and allow your eyes to function as an infinite canvas. Adorn your eyes with the captivating shades of Azul to enhance your cosmetic game.

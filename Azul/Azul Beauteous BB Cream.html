Product Specifications



Features:

1.Lightweight Coverage: The Azul Beauteous BB Cream blurs blemishes, evens out skin tone, and smooths the surface so that makeup can be applied smoothly.

2.Hydrating Formula: Packed with of hydrating ingredients, this BB cream gives your skin a quick moisture boost, leaving it feeling nourished and silky all day.

3.Azul Beauteous BB Cream's SPF formulation offers a layer of sun protection, protecting your skin from damaging UV radiation and external factors.

Ingredients:

1.Hyaluronic Acid: Azul Beauteous BB Cream, which is enhanced with hyaluronic acid, plumps and moisturizes the skin to minimize the look of fine wrinkles and to encourage a more youthful complexion.

2.Vitamin C: Including Vitamin C helps to reduce dark spots and uneven pigmentation, giving the skin a brighter, more vibrant tone.

3.Green Tea Extract: This BB cream, which contains green tea extract, provides antioxidant properties that shield your skin from free radical damage and give it a radiant, healthy appearance.

How to use:

1.First, make sure your nails are dry and clean. After removing any previous polish, trim and shape your nails to the proper length.

1.Prepare Your Skin: Wash and moisturize your face first.

2.Apply the BB Cream: Using your fingertips or a makeup sponge, lightly pump a tiny quantity of Azul Beauteous BB Cream onto your face. Then, blend it in evenly, working your way outward from the centre.

3.Build for Coverage: Apply extra BB cream to areas that could require more care for better coverage.

4.Set if Desired: For a matte look, you can choose to set the BB cream with a translucent powder.

Product Description

Azul Classic Primer, the necessary foundation for a great and long-lasting finish, will elevate your makeup routine to perfection. This silky-smooth primer is your go-to tool for building a flawless base that keeps your makeup looking wearable all day. Discover Azul Classic Primer's timeless beauty.


Product Specifications



Features:

1.Pore-Blurring Formula: The pore-blurring formula of Azul Classic Primer subtly reduces the visibility of pores, fine wrinkles, and flaws, leaving a polished and smooth surface for makeup application.

2.Extended Wear: Azul Classic Primer's breathable and lightweight composition keeps your makeup looking fresh and polished for longer while also extending its wear and preventing it from settling into fine wrinkles.

3.Hydrating Base: Packed full of hydrating elements, this primer leaves the skin feeling nourished and hydrated, which improves the application and durability of makeup.

Ingredients:

1.Hyaluronic Acid: Azul Classic Primer's hyaluronic acid enrichment gives the skin a quick moisture boost, plumping it up and encouraging a radiant complexion.

2.Vitamin E: Adding vitamin E to the mix has antioxidant properties that shield the skin from outside aggressors and encourage a supple, smooth texture.

3.Formulated Without Silicones: Azul Classic Primer has a lightweight feel without blocking pores because it is made without silicones. Fits all skin types, including those with sensitive skin.

How to use:

1.Prepare Your Skin: Start with hydrated, clean skin.

2.Apply primer: Using your fingertips, carefully distribute a tiny quantity of Azul Classic Primer over your face, paying particular attention to the areas where makeup has been applied.

3.Let It Set: Give the primer a minute to work its magic before putting on foundation and other cosmetics.

4.Personalize Your Routine: Azul Classic Primer fits right into your cosmetic regimen. Apply it as a base for your preferred foundation or on its own for a polished, natural look.

Packaging:

Azul Classic Primer is applied precisely and neatly thanks to its convenient pump bottle design. Its portable shape makes it an indispensable beauty companion for usage at home and on the move. It fits perfectly in your makeup bag or on your dressing table. Uncover a flawless canvas for your makeup creation as you explore the classic elegance of Azul Classic Primer.

Product Description

Indulge your lips in the luxurious embrace of VelvetVivid Orchid Bliss, a sublime fusion of opulence and vibrancy. Immerse yourself in the lush beauty of orchid tones that dance with vivid pigments, creating a mesmerizing and bold statement.

Product Specifications

Features:

  • Velvet Texture: Experience the velvety-smooth texture that glides effortlessly, enveloping your lips in a rich, indulgent feel.

  • Vivid Orchid Hue: The enchanting orchid hue is carefully crafted to be intensely pigmented, providing a striking and long-lasting color payoff.

  • Moisture Lock Technology: Formulated with advanced moisture-lock technology, VelvetVivid Orchid Bliss keeps your lips hydrated and supple throughout the day, preventing dryness and flaking.

  • Versatile Wear: Whether you're heading to a glamorous evening event or a casual day out, this lipstick effortlessly transitions from day to night, complementing any look with its versatile appeal.

Key Ingredients:

  • Orchid Extract: Extracted from the rarest orchids, this key ingredient adds a touch of luxury while contributing to the lipstick's smooth application.

  • Jojoba Oil: Known for its moisturizing properties, jojoba oil helps keep your lips hydrated, ensuring a comfortable wear.

  • Vivid Pigments: Carefully selected vivid pigments create a bold and captivating color that lasts, making a statement wherever you go.

How to use:

Apply VelvetVivid Orchid Bliss directly to your lips, starting from the center and working your way outward for precise and even coverage. For an intensified look, layer the lipstick as desired.

Packaging:

The sleek and sophisticated packaging of VelvetVivid Orchid Bliss reflects the elegance within. Housed in a chic tube adorned with orchid-inspired details, it's a true embodiment of luxury.

Cruelty-Free:

We are proud to say that VelvetVivid Orchid Bliss is cruelty-free, ensuring that you can enjoy its beauty with a clear conscience.

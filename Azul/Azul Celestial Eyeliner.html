Product Description

With Azul Celestial Eyeliner, a celestial-inspired eyeliner that gives your gaze a hint of magic and definition, you can take your eye makeup to new heights. Discover your creative side and craft eye-catching styles with this precision tool that applies smoothly and leaves a bold, durable finish.

Product Specifications

Features:

1.Rich, vivid colour is delivered with Azul Celestial Eyeliner in only one stroke, giving you the ability to create a dramatic, well-defined eye appearance.

2.Waterproof Formula: Thanks to the waterproof formula, which resists humidity, rips, and regular wear, you may enjoy eyeliner that stays in place all day and night.

3.Easy Application: The eyeliner's creamy, silky texture makes it easy to apply without straining or pulling, making it simple to draw fine lines and complex designs.

4.adaptable accuracy: The fine-tip applicator offers adaptable accuracy for countless creative options, whether you desire a dramatic cat-eye or a sleek line.

Ingredients:

1.Vitamin E: Azul Celestial Eyeliner, which is enhanced with vitamin E, not only prolongs the wear of the product but also nourishes the sensitive skin around the eyes, making them feel comfortable and well-cared for.

How to use:

1.Before using, give the formula a good shake.

2.Apply Azul Celestial Eyeliner sparingly down the lash line, spreading outward from the inner corner.

3.Extend the line just past the outer corner of the eye and angle it upwards to create the appearance of wings.

4.To get the intensity and style you want, play about with thickness and form.

Packaging:

The elegant, celestial-themed packaging for Azul Celestial Eyeliner captures the enchanted essence of the product. Its compact size makes it lightweight and ideal for touch-ups while on the road. A tight cap keeps the fine-tip applicator in place and guarantees the product's freshness and endurance. Accept the enchantment of Azul Celestial Eyeliner and allow it to create a celestial glow for your eyes.

Product Specifications

Features:

1.Artisan Craftsmanship: Skillfully produced by skilled artisans, each pair of Azul Enticing Fake Eyelashes ensures a natural and beautifully blended appearance for a captivating stare.

2.Luxurious Comfort: Feel feather-light comfort with our lashes, which are expertly crafted to be lightweight and effortless to apply, giving the impression that they are an extension of your own lashes.

3.Versatile Styles: Select from a variety of looks that range from voluminous and bold for dramatic evening allure to subtle and fluttery for daytime elegance.

4.Reusable Design: Azul Enticing Fake Eyelashes are made with sustainability and unbeatable value in mind. With the right maintenance, they can be used repeatedly.

Ingredients:

1.Latex-Free Adhesive: Our lashes are attached to the eye without irritating even the most delicate eyes thanks to a soft, latex-free adhesive.

How to use:

1.Using tweezers or your fingertips, carefully remove the lashes from the tray, taking care not to harm the thin lash strands.

2.If necessary, trim the lashes from the outer edge to fit your eye shape. This will ensure that the fit is exactly right.

3.The accompanying latex-free glue should be applied thinly to the lash band, then allowed to become tacky for a few seconds.

4.**Place the lashes gently along your natural lash line, pressing toward the outside corner from the inner corner.

5.For a seamless and secure finish, make any necessary positioning adjustments and let the adhesive to dry completely.
